anyways i went to hackprinceton fall with [leah](https://devpost.com/Leahie) and [joy](https://devpost.com/joywang0222), two friends from cornell. we built [Ithaca](https://devpost.com/software/ithaca-oiqtaz), a more immersive homebuying experience.

we took images from virtual tour of houses (using [matterport](https://matterport.com/)) and tried to re-create the entire house in 3D (think gaussian splatting) with segemented + labeled furniture objects and high quality textures. we ran in to a lot of computer vision/3d modeling problems that are pretty interesting and worth researching further, though it was rather detrimental and stressful for our hackathon.
