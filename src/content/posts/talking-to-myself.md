i spend a lot of time talking to myself. on my walks, laying on my bed, eating dinner, doing anything alone. in fact i am audibly speaking as i write this post.

maybe this is a common event for people (or maybe not, i dont really know) but i find it very fun to do weird accents and listen to myself with those accents.

i’m a big fan of pretending to be one of the three of the original top gear narrators (clarkson, hammond, may) since i remember their voices very distinctly from watching top gear growing up.

i talk to myself about everything! my ideas, memories (while reminiscing), my thoughts on interactions i had today.

is this normal? i dont know… i’ve always done this growing up… 🤷‍♂
