Quick post today, just an update that I passed my Technician-level ham radio licensing exam and will soon be able to operate ham radio! I've got a Baofeng radio at home; My parents have kindly agreed to bring it up to Cornell next week when they come and visit. Hopefully I will get my callsign by then so I can start making contacts around Cornell and Ithaca.

Big thanks to [W2CXM, the Cornell Amateur Radio Club](https://w2cxm.org/) for providing free examinations and licenses, as well as for their support in helping me study/prepare. They have a really cool space up on Barton Hall and have some great stuff planned for this semester; I would highly recommend any Cornell student with even a marginal interest in radio/EE to come to a meeting!
