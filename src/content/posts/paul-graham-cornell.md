Yesterday Paul Graham (Founder of YC, Cornell '86) came to speak at Cornell University yesterday. I was a part of his larger talk to several hundred interested Cornell students and a smaller private QnA session hosted by [Armada](https://armada.build/) for active Armada members, project team leads, and a few other students interested in tech.

His larger talk centered around how to build a _formidable_ startup and the reasons to _stay in university_ while doing so. I'll summarize his speech in the following bullet points:

- Stay in school!
  - There is no better place to harness your natural curiosity and learn anything you want with freedom than university
  - Being at university (especially as an undergrad) means 4 years of continuous, unbounded learning that is partially funded... by the university itself


So... what should you do?
- Well, stay in school, duh...
- Work on cool side projects
- Work on anything that sparks your interest
- Work with clubs doing cool things
- Learn things outside of your intended major
- `#staycurious`
