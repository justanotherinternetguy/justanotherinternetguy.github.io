Like many other people my age, I was a pretty hardcore gamer during COVID-19. I still love video games, but I have mostly moved on to bigger things. I question _why_ I was so hooked to the games I played. What did I truly enjoy about these games? A nagging thought at the back of my mind always wondered if I can transform my life into a video game; to allow my mind to see my body as nothing but an avatar in a game of Life. Perhaps this will give me the motivation grind life, just like how I grind video games.

For reference, I almost exclusively played competitive, multiplayer games with PvP combat. I played a lot of shooters and FPS's (CS:GO/2, Arsenal, Krunker) as well as MMORPGs (Elite Dangerous, Runescape). I also poured thousands of hours into competitive Minecraft PvP (Bedwars, Duels, Skywars, the lot).
